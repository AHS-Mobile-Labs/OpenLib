PRIVACY POLICY - OpenLib
Last Updated: June 9, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION
OpenLib ("we", "us", "our") operates the OpenLib web application at
https://www.openlib.online. This Privacy Policy explains what information
we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when you use
OpenLib.

OpenLib is a community-curated directory for discovering open-source
software. Public app listings, reviews, edit requests, reports, and other
community activity may be visible to other users.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

a) Account Information
When you sign in with Google or GitHub, Firebase Authentication provides
your display name, email address, profile photo URL, authentication
provider, linked providers, and unique user ID. We do not receive or store
your password.

b) Profile, Organization, and Team Information
If you create or update a profile, organization, or team-related record, we
may store the information you provide, such as display name, bio, avatar,
organization name, organization description, role, verification status, and
team or organization membership data.

c) User-Generated Content
We store content you choose to submit, including app submissions, app
metadata, reviews, ratings, comments, edit requests, report details,
ownership claims, moderation discussions, screenshots, logos, and other
files or text you provide.

d) App Interaction Data
We store interaction data used to operate OpenLib features, including
likes, dislikes, bookmarks, follows, review votes, helpful votes, app page
views, source link clicks, download clicks, open-app clicks, and related
activity signals used for rankings, recommendations, and abuse prevention.

e) Usage and Technical Data
OpenLib may collect basic technical and usage data such as page views,
session data, browser type, device type, referrer, approximate location,
timestamps, and error or performance information. We use Google Analytics 4
on production hostnames only. We do not use advertising trackers.

f) Local Browser Storage
OpenLib may use localStorage, browser cache, and similar browser storage to
remember preferences, theme, cached app data, update checks, session
tracking keys, and temporary UI state.

3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use information to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve OpenLib
- Authenticate users and manage accounts
- Display public profiles, reviews, submissions, comments, and edit history
- Review app submissions, reports, ownership claims, and edit requests
- Operate rankings, trending lists, recommendations, bookmarks, and votes
- Detect abuse, spam, security issues, and policy violations
- Preserve audit history for moderation, edits, and app version changes
- Measure aggregate usage and performance
- Communicate service changes through the site or repository

4. PUBLIC CONTENT

Some information is public by design. Public content may include your
display name, profile photo, bio, reviews, comments, app submissions, edit
requests, approvals, merged changes, team or organization profile data,
and other community contributions. Do not submit information you do not
want displayed publicly.

5. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

OpenLib uses Firebase Authentication, Google Cloud Firestore, Firebase
Storage, Firebase Hosting, and Firebase/Google Cloud Functions. Data is
stored on Google Cloud infrastructure and is protected by transport
encryption, cloud platform security controls, and Firestore/Storage
security rules.

No system can be guaranteed completely secure. You are responsible for
keeping access to your Google or GitHub account secure.

6. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

OpenLib may use or link to:
- Firebase Authentication for Google and GitHub sign-in
- Google Cloud Firestore for database storage
- Firebase Storage for uploaded logos and screenshots
- Firebase Hosting and Cloud Functions for hosting and prerendering
- Google Analytics 4 for aggregate analytics
- GitHub for repository links, issue reporting, and source-code references
- YouTube embeds when an app listing includes a video
- External app websites, download pages, documentation, and source links

Third-party websites and services have their own privacy policies. OpenLib
is not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

7. COOKIES AND ANALYTICS

Firebase and authentication providers may use cookies or similar
technologies for sign-in and security. Google Analytics may use cookies or
similar identifiers for aggregate analytics. You can limit cookies through
your browser settings, and you can use browser tools or extensions to
block analytics.

8. DATA SHARING

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:
- With service providers that operate OpenLib infrastructure
- When content is public by design
- To comply with law, legal process, or enforceable requests
- To protect OpenLib, users, contributors, or the public from abuse,
  security threats, or fraud

9. RETENTION AND DELETION

We keep information for as long as needed to operate OpenLib, preserve
community audit history, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes,
and prevent abuse. You may request deletion of your account or associated
personal data by contacting us. Some public or moderation records may be
retained when necessary for security, integrity, legal compliance, or
historical audit purposes.

10. YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export,
delete, or object to certain processing of your personal information. You
can view or update some account and profile information in OpenLib. For
requests that cannot be completed through the UI, contact us through the
GitHub issue tracker.

11. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

OpenLib is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has
provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it
where appropriate.

12. INTERNATIONAL USE

OpenLib may be accessed from different countries, and information may be
processed in countries where Google, Firebase, GitHub, or other service
providers operate. By using OpenLib, you understand that information may be
processed outside your country of residence.

13. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be
posted on this page and in privacy.txt with an updated "Last Updated" date.
Continued use of OpenLib after changes means you acknowledge the updated
policy.

14. CONTACT

For privacy-related questions or requests, open an issue at:
https://github.com/AHS-Mobile-Labs/OpenLib/issues

You can also visit the repository:
https://github.com/AHS-Mobile-Labs/OpenLib
